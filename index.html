Profile

I am an anthropologist working on human rights, political violence, and climate-induced displacement in Bangladesh. My research is qualitative and documentary: reading across testimony, media investigations, NGO records, and legal documents to reconstruct what happened to people and why it matters. Alongside research I edit a youth literary magazine, teach humanities to school students, and help run a youth-led education initiative. My current interests are transitional justice, enforced disappearance, and the anthropology of collective violence.
